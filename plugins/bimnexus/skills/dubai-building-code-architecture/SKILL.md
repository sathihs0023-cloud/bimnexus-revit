# Dubai Building Code (DBC) 2021 – General, Architecture, Vertical Transportation, Security

Condensed study notes of DBC 2021 Edition Parts A, B, D and J (non-villa buildings). Villas/townhouses → use the `dubai-building-code-villas` skill. Accessibility, envelope, structure, utilities and MEP → use `dubai-building-code-technical`.

## How to use this skill
1. Identify occupancy (Assembly, Business, Educational, Hotel, Residential, Parking, Retail, Mall, Industrial, Storage; healthcare → DHA) and whether the project is new, renovation, modification, reconstruction, change of use or addition (A.5).
2. Check hierarchy before quoting a number: plot DCR / affection plan overrides Part B planning defaults (setbacks, height, parking, projections). UAE FLSC 2018 governs fire/egress (exit widths, exit numbers, travel distance). DHA governs healthcare; DTCM governs hotel star criteria; IACAD for mosques; RTA for access; DCAA for heights in flight paths.
3. Quote the clause/table reference (e.g. "DBC B.5.2, Table B.3") so the user can verify.
4. These are paraphrased summaries for professional reference — always tell the user to confirm against the official latest DBC edition/addenda and the Authority (DM, DDA, Trakhees, DSOA) before submission. Do not reproduce the code text verbatim.
5. For compliance reviews, produce a checklist table: requirement | DBC ref | design value | compliant? | note.

## Part A – General
- DBC + UAE FLSC = main regs. DBC replaces DM, DDA, Trakhees, DSOA building-design regs; DEWA/IACAD incorporated; SIRA summarised in Part J; TRA in G.11.
- International standards: latest edition unless DBC names one (e.g. ASCE/SEI 7-16). DBC vs reference standard conflict → most restrictive. General vs specific → specific applies.
- Shall = mandatory; Should = recommended; May = permitted.
- Existing buildings: renovation/modification → DBC+FLSC apply to the works; reconstruction & change of use → also to impacted areas (stairs, lifts, sanitary); addition → also assess existing building if addition relies on it for escape/VT.
- Not covered: infrastructure (bridges, tunnels, metro stations, utilities, power stations, masts), marine, oil & gas, temporary structures, stadia/grandstands/silos/chimneys, historical buildings. Heritage-area buildings DO follow DBC (heritage dept controls elevations).
- Adoption: projects with final approved design are exempt; others comply.
- Alternative solutions (A.8): only where prescriptive is not reasonably achievable or (existing) cost is disproportionate; must meet performance statements and be at least equivalent; holistic; Authority decides; burden on Consultant.
- GFA (Table A.3; to external face of external walls, centreline of party walls, excl. cladding): INCLUDED – habitable/occupiable incl. toilets, mezzanines, lobbies, corridors, stairs, storage at/above ground, base of atria, lift shaft at recall floor, non-villa basement habitable/occupiable areas, gym area exceeding 50% of roof area. EXCLUDED – prayer & ablution, gym ≤50% of roof area, pools + their services, villa basements, open non-AC arcades, attics <2.15 m (services only), voids, parking & parking lobbies, terraces, balconies, internal roads, loading, MEP rooms/plant floors, shafts/ducts, lift shafts at other floors, lift machine rooms, refuse chutes, waste rooms, storage below ground. DCR-stated GFA prevails.
- Built-up area: incl. balconies, terraces, projections, covered parking, loading, basements, service floors, indoor pools; excl. shafts/courtyards, uncovered landscape, pergolas/standalone canopies.
- GA = inside face of external walls incl. corridors, stairs, walls, columns, excl. shafts & courtyards (occupant load, lift exemptions). NA = occupied area excl. corridors, stairs, toilets, MEP, closets (occupant load, parking).
- Check height separately against DBC, DCR, FLSC and DCAA definitions. Coordinates DLTM; levelling datum Port Rashid. Dimensions not marked min/max are absolute.

## Part B – Architecture
### Development limits (B.4)
- Gate level ≥ +300 mm above road edge or 2–5% slope road→plot access.
- Building height = road edge level at main access → mean roof FFL; total height → highest element. Allowable height = permitted floors × 6 m (G+10 → 66 m). May exceed (if DCAA/DCR allow): minarets/domes, chimneys, tanks, lift overruns, stairs, aerials, wind towers, pergolas, helipads (DCAA), roof shading, compliant adverts.
- Default setbacks (Table B.1; only if DCR silent) from neighbour: basement 0; G to G+3 3 m; G+4 3.75; G+5 4.5; G+6 5.25; G+7 6.0; G+8 6.75; G+9+ 7.5 m. Road side 0. Retail-to-retail zero setback for retail floors. Sikka: measure from sikka centre (credit max 3 m if sikka >6 m); if result ≤1.5 m no setback. Cemetery ≥3 m. Metro: RTA railway protection code. Buildings on same plot: sum of both neighbour setbacks (≥3 m for G-only).
- Allowed in setback: boundary wall, landscape, RMU/substations, basement/substructure, pergolas & light parking shades, uncovered parking/roads, guard rooms, retail-to-retail.
- Setbacks must also satisfy FLSC external fire spread & fire access; tower on podium >30 m from fire access → fire truck access onto podium.
- Projections/balconies: ≥3 m above sidewalk; ≥0.6 m back from road edge or ≥5.5 m clear above road; ≥4.5 m over fire access; over ROW only if road >9 m, ≤10% of street width and ≤1.8 m; keep neighbour setbacks; privacy partitions between units' balconies.
- Guardrails (drop >760 mm): ≥1,200 mm high; 100 mm sphere; no climbable elements within 865 mm of base; bottom gap ≤100 mm; ASCE 7-16 loads; glass per E.9.2. Balcony doors not self-closing/latching/locking.
- Plot coverage excludes canopies, parking shades, pergolas, eaves, non-accessible projections.
- Ground-floor exits separated ≥1/3 of building diagonal (sprinklered) or 1/2 (unsprinklered). Emergency command centre (malls, amusement parks, high-rise, super high-rise): ground floor, 1 h fire barrier, entry from outside on fire access or main lobby, ≥19 m², min dim 3 m.
- Basement within plot; soffit ≤1.2 m above gate level. Podium habitable spaces treated as indoor (envelope/acoustic/HVAC).
- Roof structures ≤50% of roof area and ≥1.5 m from envelope (stair/lift rooms exempt from setback but count in 50%), otherwise counted as a floor.
### Space (B.5)
- Occupant load factors (m²/person; exits still per FLSC Table 3.13): ballroom/multipurpose 0.65 NA; standing waiting 0.65; restaurants/dining/seated waiting/conference/exhibition/food court/stages 1.4 NA; prayer 0.9 NA; pool water 4.6 GA, deck 2.8; gym no equip 1.4, with equip 4.6; gaming 1.0; kitchens 9.3; library reading 4.6, stacks 9.3; museums 5.0; airport waiting 1.4, baggage 1.9; enclosed office 9.3 GA, open office 4.6 GA; retail 5.6 GA; department store 3.7; goods-only floors 27.9; mall <14,000 m² 2.8 GLA, >14,000 3.3 GLA; factory 9.3; warehouse 27.9; classrooms 1.9 NA; labs 4.6; shared sleeping 5.0; labour sleeping 3.7; bench 455 mm/person.
- Min room sizes (Table B.3, net area / min dimension): bedroom & living 10.5 m² / 3 m; studio 21 m² (incl. bath+kitchen) / 3 m; housekeeper/guard (single, excl. toilet) 4.5 m² / 2.1 m; retail shop – / 2.4 m; showroom – / 9 m; enclosed office – / 2.4 m; enclosed kitchen 5.4 m² / 1.8 m; emergency command centre 19 m² / 3 m. Kitchen clearance between counters ≥1.2 m. Hotels → DTCM; healthcare → DHA.
- Min clear heights (Table B.4): Awqat mosque 3.8 m; Jumaa mosque 5.0; prayer rooms 2.7; ballrooms 2.7; offices 2.5; retail/showrooms 2.5; classrooms 2.7; residential & hotel living/bedrooms 2.7; parking 2.3; other occupiable, toilets, storage, corridors, lobbies, egress 2.3; fire pump room 2.5; stairs 2.03 over treads / 2.3 over landings. Protrusions allowed in ≤50% of ceiling with ≥2.03 m headroom.
### Circulation (B.6)
- Level changes 13–535 mm in egress → ramp (C.5.9.1) or stair.
- Corridor clear widths (Table B.5): assembly/public 1.5 single-loaded / 1.8 double; residential common 1.5 single-sided / 1.8 double-sided; inside apartments 1.0; retail 1.8; mall/retail >3,600 m² GA per floor 3 m single-sided / 6 m double-sided; educational 2.1 single / 3.0 double; offices 1.5 / 1.8; hotels 1.5 / 1.8 (+DTCM); service corridors 1.2. Retail >3,600 m² GA at ground not fire separated = mall (FLSC).
- Mall kiosks: ≥3 m from shopfronts, ≥6 m apart, ≤18 m² (28 m² open mall).
- Stairs: number/width/separation/FR per FLSC Ch.3. Exit stairs RCC where FLSC requires (2 h blockwork OK for low-rise / low-depth underground). ≥1 protected stair to every roof (and to LPG tank / PV roof; no ladders there). Clear width ≥1,200 mm (handrails may encroach 100 mm each side). Riser 100–180 mm; tread ≥280; ≤10 mm variation per flight; ≤2% tread slope; max rise between landings 3,660 mm; landing ≥ stair width (needn't exceed 1,200 on straight run); door swing ≤½ landing.
- Regular-use and external steps: tread ≥300, riser 150–165, 2–14 risers per flight; nosing contrast ≥30 LRV; ≥200 lux; warning surface at top recommended.
- Handrails both sides at 865–965 mm (≤1,065 when part of guard); extra 650–750 mm rail on regular-use/external stairs; Ø32–51 mm (non-circular perimeter 100–160, max 57); 57 mm wall gap; 305 mm extensions; every part of egress width within 760 mm of a rail (intermediate rails leave ≥760); continuous at inner landings; 30 LRV; not sun-hot.
- Stair guards ≥1,200 (1,065 if handrail-guard); 100 mm sphere up to 865 mm; 150 mm triangle at riser/tread.
- Open external exit stair max 15 m above ground; >11 m high → 1,200 mm opaque screen. Stair ID signs (floor, wing, direction; Arabic + English) at floor landings, 1,220–2,135 mm, letters ≥25 mm. Scissor stair = one exit.
- Winders/spiral/curved not in egress except industrial equipment, storage mezzanines, retail goods mezzanines. Winders ≥150 narrow / ≥280 at 305 line; spiral width ≥660, headroom 2,030, riser ≤240, tread ≥190 at 305; curved ≥255 at 305. Industrial access stair width ≥560, tread ≥255, riser ≤230.
- Exit stair nosing strips: integral/painted (no tape), 25–51 mm, ≤13 mm from edge.
- Doors (Table B.7): entry ≥915 mm clear; bathrooms ≥815; exit doors ≥915 or per FLSC occupant load; ≥1.2 m between adjacent doors; swing/hardware/FR per FLSC 3.2.
- Windows (Table B.8): net glazing ≥10% of floor area for residential/hotel living & bedrooms (incl. housekeeper rooms), classrooms, mosques, office workstation areas. Openers 900–1,200 mm. Skylights ≥15° count. Daylight modelling alternative ≥150 lux bedrooms/living. Room depth ≤3 × window-wall width. Views to outside required for business, residential, educational, hotel occupiable spaces. Windows facing neighbours only with setback + FLSC fire spread compliance.
- Atria: 1 h separation unless engineered smoke control. Courtyards ≥6 × 6 m, ≤23 m high; >15 m high → building fully sprinklered; smaller only with rated walls; may be covered if 50% perforated. Light wells for habitable rooms only if fully fire-rated with fixed rated glazing.
### Access & vehicles (B.7)
- Separate residential entrances/lobbies from commercial in mixed use. Drop-off per RTA Access Management Manual 7.2. TIA/TIS when Authority requires. Vehicle access ≥15 m from chamfered corner at intersections/T-junctions.
- Internal roads ≥3 m one-way, ≥6 m two-way; lit to CIE 115 M5; all manoeuvring within plot.
- Vehicle ramps (Table B.9): straight ≤12%, lane ≥3 m, headroom 2.4 m; curved ≤12%, 3.5 m, inner R ≥4 m; helical ≤8%, 5 m, inner R ≥6 m. Transitions half slope: 12%→6% over 3.6 m; 10%→5% 3 m; 8%→4% 2.4 m. Lane curbs ≥300 × 150. Ramps not egress (except one level from discharge without shutters).
- Mechanical parking: TIA, BS EN 14010 certificate, no occupant access, AC waiting area, 25% capacity on standby power, FLSC.
- Parking: separate from other uses; vehicle barriers ≥1,200 mm where drop >300 mm (ASCE 7-16 4.5.3). Open parking = openings ≥20% of perimeter wall area and ≥40% of perimeter length on ≥2 sides (NFPA 88A); else enclosed + mechanical ventilation. Parking slope ≤4% (accessible ≤2%). 6 m straight approach before barriers; wheel stops ≤120 high; dead-end aisles ≤8 bays; tandem: same residential unit max 2, hotel valet max 3.
- Bays (Table B.11): 2.5 × 5.5 m; parallel 2.5 × 6.0 (aisle 3.0); aisles 45° 3.3, 60° 3.8, 75° 5.5, 90° one-way 5.5, two-way 6.0. 90° bays may be 2.5 × 5.0 for all bays on plots <30 m long, or ≤10% of bays otherwise. +300 mm where walls/columns obstruct. Bus bays (12 m bus) 4 × 13 m.
- Parking ratios (Table B.13; DCR prevails): studio/1BR/2BR 1 per unit; 3–4BR 2; 5BR/duplex/penthouse 3; staff accommodation 1/450 m² NA + 1 bus/650 m²; student 1/40 residents; office & government 1/50 m² NA; retail 1/70 m² NA; mall 1/50 m² NA; cinema greater of 1/3 seats or 1/20 m² viewing NA; exhibition 1/50; mosque 1/18 worshippers (residential areas) or 1/15 (centres/industrial); library 1/50; nursery/KG/school 1 per classroom (+1 bus per 3 classrooms for KG/school); university 1/50 m² classroom NA +10% visitors +1 bus/200 students; hotel 1/5 rooms, 1/2 suites; hotel apartment 1 per apt ≤150 m², 2 if larger; hotel F&B/retail/office 1/50; ballroom/function 1/20 m² NA; healthcare 1 per bed + 1/50 m² NA; industry/warehouse 1/70 m² office NA; petrol station 2. Unlisted → RTA rates.
- Preferred (EV/hybrid/carpool) bays 5% when >20 bays; malls: EV chargers on 20% of preferred bays. Bicycles: student/labour accommodation 10% of occupants; others 5% of car requirement; ≤30 m from entrance, shaded.
- Loading: within plot, rear/side, drained, lit; bay 4 × 4 m, headroom 4.5 m (12 m truck) / 5 m (15–20 m); heavy-vehicle driveway 9 m two-way / 4.5 m one-way; hotels & malls 1 bay for 12 m truck; industrial 15 m truck; others 9 m truck.
- Service ramps ≥1.2 m wide, ≤8%, ≤10 m runs. Service ladders only for maintenance spaces/unoccupied roofs: rails 900 mm above top, cage/lifeline >6 m, width ≥800 (Table B.17). Fire pump room at basement/ground within 6 m sight of protected stair; no ladders/hatches.
### Communal provisions (B.8)
- No unisex common toilets (except family/accessible). Wet finishes: 100 mm coved skirting; impervious walls to 1,200 mm within 600 mm of fixtures; showers to 1,800 mm.
- Sanitary fixtures (Table B.18; per gender = half occupants rounded up; sum fractions for mixed use; WCs and basins): restaurants M 1/75 first 300 then 1/200, F 1/50 first 200 then 1/75; ballrooms/exhibition/museums/gyms/waiting/cinemas/conference/library M 1/125, F 1/65; amusement/play/skating WC M 1/75 to 1,500 then 1/120, F 1/40 to 1,600 then 1/60 (basins M 1/120, F 1/60); offices 1/20 first 100 then 1/50; retail/malls M 1/200, F 1/100 first 500 then 1/200; factories/warehouses 1/25; schools & KG students 1 per class (child-size), staff 1 per 10 classes (min 2 per gender); nurseries 1 per baby room; labour accommodation 1/10 WC + 1/10 basin + 1/10 shower; student accommodation 1/10 + shower 1/8; fuel stations public 1M + 1F, staff as offices; hotels DTCM; healthcare DHA; unlisted → IBC. Urinals may replace ≤33% of male WCs. Every dwelling ≥1 bathroom.
- Mosques/prayer rooms: WC 1/100 per gender, basins 1/100, ablution 1/50 worshippers. Pools: WC M 1/75 (→1/120 above 1,500), F 1/40 (→1/60 above 1,600); basins M 1/200, F 1/150; showers 1/10 (residents' pools −50%, min 1 per gender).
- Toilet stalls 900 × 1,500 (in-swing) / 900 × 1,250 (out-swing); toilet corridor ≥1.05 m; partitions 100–150 mm off floor, ≥2,000 high; urinal screens bottom ≤300, top ≥1,500, ≥500 deep. Fixture centres ≥400 from walls, 750 between; 750 clear in front; 600 to opposite fixture. Cleaner's room ≥1.5 m² with sink in buildings >100 m² GA.
- Drinking fountains (not in toilets): offices, schools, KG, nurseries, labour & student accommodation 1/100; ballrooms/museums/cinemas/factories 1/500; restaurants (if water not served), amusement, pools, retail/malls 1/1,000; mosques per IACAD.
- Feeding rooms: malls & amusement parks >1,000 m² GA or >250 occupants; within 300 m of any point; ≥4.5 m², ≥2.1 m each way; accessible. First aid per DM H&S guidelines.
- Gyms acoustically isolated (H.10.3.3); changing per gender.
- Pools (DM PH&SD guides): fence ≥1,200 high, ≤50 mm bottom gap, 100 mm sphere, self-closing/latching lockable gates opening away, fence ≥1,200 from plot limit (exemptions: building walls with alarmed/self-closing doors, ASTM F1346 cover, natural water body, full-time lifeguards); windows by pool sill ≥1,200; deck ≥1.2 m at 2%; ladders/steps if >600 deep, one per 30 m perimeter; regular steps only if ≤1.0 m deep (tread ≥250 × 600, riser ≤300); diving: 5 m clear above, depth 2.5 m (+0.3 m per m above 1 m), 3 m spacing; deck >30 m to exit → 2 exits.
- Prayer rooms (malls, business, hotels; staff/labour accommodation): capacity 8% of occupants (<300) or 5% of occupants beyond first 300; 50/50 M/F unless justified; separate entrances; ablution; toilets not in Qibla direction or facing prayer space.
- Service rooms: RMUs not in basements; transformer/LV/HV/generator rooms preferably ground (basement allowed per G.7.6 + FLSC). Basement waste room: 1 h, B1 only, 10 ACH dedicated extract.
- Waste: room at ground or B1 (with service lift); outdoor enclosure only if largest floor GA <250 m² (≥2.5 m horizontal / 5 m vertical from living-room windows, roofed, concrete pad); access ≥1.2 m, ≤1:12; manual bin moves ≤1,000 l max 7 m, ≤360 l max 50 m, avoid >1,500 l; store 2 days. Generation: residential & commercial 12 kg/100 m² NA, office 5 kg/100 m², hotel 3 kg/room, 5 kg/suite. GA 230–460 m² → 1.2 × 1.8 m room; >460 m² → room for all bins. Containers: ≤100 kg 1×1.1 m³; ≤250 kg 1×2.5; ≤500 kg 2×2.5; ≤750 kg 3×2.5; ≤1,000 kg 4×2.5; above → consultant. Recycling sort area +5 m²; bulky waste ≥5 m².
- Refuse chutes: residential >G+3 with any floor >250 m²; Ø ≥600 (BS 1703), non-combustible, cleaning & fire protection; access area 900 × 1,000 with ≥915 out-swing self-closing door; recycling via second chute/separator or per-floor recycling rooms ≥2 m².
### Building types (B.9)
- Cinemas/theatres: FLSC 3.5.1; main entrance for 2/3 of occupants.
- Mosques (IACAD): Islamic style; minaret:mosque height ~3:1; minaret spiral stair radius ≥900; mihrab + minbar (right of mihrab); hall doors ≥3 m high at rear with vision panels; separate Imam entrance; prayer row depth 1,330 mm, 675 mm per worshipper; rectangular hall with long side perpendicular to Qibla; avoid columns. Women's hall (except industrial areas) ≥20% of worshippers, not basement, not beside men's toilets, screen at ≥2.1 m (both ground) or ≥1.2 m (women's above). Awqat: 100–300 men (80%) + 20% women; Jumaa: >300 men; >500 → secondary hall = 1/3 of men's hall with mihrab. Ablution seats, separated from toilets. Residence bedrooms ≥15 m²; cleaner studio; Imam 2BR + 3 toilets + courtyard; Jumaa adds Moazen 1BR. ≥25% eastern WCs.
- Food premises: DM food licensing conditions, PD ISO/TS 22002-2; coved impervious finishes; hoods H.4.12.15; chimneys at rear or in shafts; cold rooms openable from inside.
- Schools: DM public amenities guide + MoE; segregate vehicles/pedestrians; safe bus zone; library; labs away from classrooms. Kindergartens: ground floor only; open/play space ≥200% of classroom area, ≥30% shaded. Nurseries: ground floor only; 1,000 mm between beds; playground ≥0.6 m²/child.
- Apartments: units separated by 1 h. Labour accommodation: ≤8 per room; supervisor room per 40; no triple bunks, double bunks 0.9 m clear; 1 h between sleeping units; dining/TV for 30% at once; kitchen ≥1/3 of dining at ground; recreation ≥25% NA; water fountains ≤30 m apart; first aid room ≥10 m² per 1,000 workers. Student rooms: single ≥7 m², shared 5 m²/student (min 2.4 m), M/F segregated.
- Industrial/warehouses: offices ≤20% GA; separate heavy/light vehicle access if >1 truck trip per 100 m³ GA at peak; doors ≥2 m from road edge; multi-tenant warehouses 1 h separation.
- Fuel stations: FLSC Ch.13; entry/exit 6 m, R ≥10 m; between islands ≥5.5 m (1 pump) / 8 m (2 pumps); highway stations prayer rooms for 15% of peak users.
### Outdoor & sustainability (B.10)
- Walk-off entry system full width × 3 m long at regular entrances. Screen rooftop plant/refuse. Fences ≤3 m. Smoking areas ≥7.5 m from entrances, operable windows, intakes.
- ≥25% of planted area native/adaptive; pedestrian links in plot shaded (shade SRI ≥29 steep / ≥78 flat); ≥50% of hardscape SRI ≥33, open grid, tree/PV/high-SRI shaded.
- Materials: recycled content ≥10% of value (post-consumer + ½ pre-consumer); regional ≥10% (within 800 km); no asbestos; lead/heavy metals within DM limits (DCL-certified); ≥25% certified timber by volume; urea-formaldehyde limits. EIA for industrial, hazardous-waste generators, marinas, near protected areas or when requested.
### Wayfinding (B.11)
- Self-locate within 70 m outdoors / 30 m indoors; orientation signs at intersections and every 30 m; building name + Makani number on façade; directory at business entrances; overhead signs ≥2,100 mm clear; English + Arabic (Arabic right-aligned); maps north-up with you-are-here; door ID signs 1,200–1,600 mm on latch/left side; digital screens 1,600–2,000 mm tilted 15–30°; text height by reading distance (1 m 14 mm min; 5 m 70; 20 m 140; ≥50 m 170).

## Part D – Vertical transportation
- One standard suite only: ASME A17 or BS EN 81. Firefighting lifts also FLSC Ch.2 s2.9 & Ch.3 s3.9.
- Energy: VVVF drives; car lights >70 lm/W, off after ≤5 min idle; standby mode; escalators slow after ≤3 min idle and stop after ≤15 min (sensors top & bottom).
- Lifts in entrance lobby near main entrance; AC lobby between lifts and enclosed parking; apartment doors not opening directly into lift lobby; hoistway noise controlled (H.10).
- Max travel lobby→furthest unit door/point: residential, hotel, open-plan office 60 m; retail/mall, assembly, education 150 m.
- Lobbies: ≤8 lifts per lobby (conventional control); ≤4 in a line; single-sided lobby ≥2.4 m deep (residential typical floors: corridor width or 1.8 m, whichever greater); facing cores ≥ max(2.4 m, 2 × deepest car, 1.5 × firefighting car depth) and ≤4.5 m.
- Method 1 (prescriptive charts by population, occupied floors, boarding floors) for single-occupancy buildings; outside chart limits or >2 groupings → VT consultant, Method 2.
- Population rates: apartments studio 1.5, 1BR 1.8, 2BR 3, 3BR 4, 4BR 5, +1 per extra bedroom/maid room; staff & student accommodation studio/1BR 2, 2BR 4, 3BR 6, +2/bedroom; labour 8 per room (lifts = greater of 1 per 1,000 workers or 1 per 2 entrances); hotels 4–5★ 1.8/room, 1–3★ 1.5/room, public areas 1.2–1.5 m²/person; hotel apartments studio 1, 1BR 1.5, 2BR 2.5, 3BR 3.5; offices chart assumes 10 m²/person on 80% GA; parking 1.5/car (3.0 in malls). Two groupings serving one zone → analyse at 120% population.
- Rated speed = travel / time: apartments 40–45 s; accommodation 50–60; hotels 1–2★ 35–40, 3★ 25–30, 4–5★ 20–25; hotel apts, offices, healthcare, retail, education 25–30 (concentrated office 30–35).
- Service lifts: residential 1 (FF or passenger lift can double with scheduled service mode); hotel 1–2★ 1 (+1 per 200 keys over 250); 3★ 2; 4–5★ 2 (+1 per 200 keys over 500); hotel apts 1; office 1; retail 1 (+1 per 100 outlets over 75).
- Firefighting lifts (FLSC): malls, multi-storey amusement parks, high-rise, super high-rise, high-depth; serve all floors; own RCC shaft (or all lifts in shared RCC shaft are FF lifts); super high-rise FF lobby ≥9 m² GA; never used as goods lifts.
- Minimum cars: residential ≤10 floors 750 kg (1,200 × 1,500 car, 900 × 2,100 door); >10 floors 1,050 kg (1,600 × 1,500, 1,100 door); residential service 1,275 kg (1,200 × 2,300 × 2,500 h). Hotels 1–2★ 1,050 kg; 3–5★ 1,350 kg; 4–5★ service 1,600 kg. Offices ≤5 floors 1,050, >5 floors 1,275. Retail passenger 1,600, trolley lifts 2,500. Car parks 1,275. Schools 2 lifts 750 kg; university classroom blocks 2 for first 400 people +1 per 300 (1,350 kg).
- Escalators 4,500 p/h, 1,000 mm steps, 35° max (≤6 m rise) else 30°, 2–3 flat steps; moving walks ≤12°, 1,000 mm pallets. Retail: escalators primary; trolleys → inclined walks; 600–4,500 people 1 escalator group + 2 lifts; 4,501–8,000 2 groups + 2 pairs; +1 group & +1 pair per extra 4,000.
- Method 2 targets: HC5% apartments 7%, student 8%, staff 6%, hotels 10–15%, hotel apts 10%, office 12% (13% lunch), healthcare/retail/parking 8–10%, universities 16–20%. Average waiting: apartments ≤40 s, staff/student ≤45, hotels 4–5★ ≤30, 3★ ≤35, offices ≤35, others ≤40. Capacity factor offices 80%, hotels 4–5★ 50%, retail 50%, panoramic ≤70%. Door times 2.2/3.2 s typical, 1.9/2.8 s for ≥120 m towers, offices and 3–5★ hotels. Bed lifts accel ≤0.6 m/s². Destination control preferred for mixed use/heavy up-peak, not for hotels. Submit D.10 summary forms/report.

## Part J – Security (SIRA Preventative Systems Manual v3.1; check latest online)
- Security designers/installers/maintainers must be SIRA-registered; verify accreditation.
- Table J.1 sets VSS (CCTV), control room, EACS, alarm/IDS and HVM needs for 23 building types: cinemas, recreation parks, art galleries, shooting ranges, places of worship, banks, internet & SIM shops, staff/labour accommodation, important complexes (tower groups, villa compounds), hotels, department stores, precious-goods shops, arms stores, vehicle rental, warehouses (general, hazardous/precious, self-storage, robotic), precious-metal & hazardous factories, malls, fuel stations.
- Notes: HVM at main gates/gathering areas of malls & recreation parks; galleries with an artwork >AED 1 M → IDS; precious goods >AED 100,000 → IDS, EACS at cashiers; 3–5★ hotels & luxury hotel apartments → digital VSS and networked EACS room locks (≥3,000 events, records ≥6 months).
- Camera coverage (Table J.2): identification/recognition at entrances, lobbies, exits, receptions, cashiers, inside lifts, ATMs; detection in corridors, perimeters, car-park driveways, pool decks; recognition at electrical, water-tank and AHU rooms and fuel/gas stores; ANPR at car-park and loading entries. Recording 31 days (90 for some high-risk types, 75 for ATMs), outdoor 25 fps; central VideoGuard monitoring (incl. residential >50 units).
- Locks/EACS on exit doors must meet FLSC Ch.3 Table 3.2.
