# Dubai Building Code (DBC) 2021 – Accessibility, Envelope, Structure, Utilities, Indoor Environment

Condensed study notes of DBC 2021 Parts C, E, F, G and H for non-villa buildings. For planning/architecture/lifts/security use `dubai-building-code-architecture`; for villas/townhouses use `dubai-building-code-villas`.

## How to use
- Cite the clause/table (e.g. "DBC E.5.2.3, Table E.5"). UAE FLSC 2018 governs fire; DEWA, TRA, district cooling providers and DHA have their own current publications that can be stricter — flag them.
- Notes are paraphrased; tell the user to verify against the official latest DBC and Authority circulars. Don't reproduce code text verbatim.
- For reviews, output a table: item | DBC ref | requirement | design | status.

## Part C – Accessibility (people of determination; BS 8300 / ADA-based)
- Applies to all new buildings and common areas/accessible units of multi-residential; not individual villas. Equal access for both genders. Emergency provisions per FLSC.
- Accessible routes: from accessible parking, drop-off, transit, streets to accessible entrance; headroom 2,100; slopes >5% are ramps; cross-fall ≤2%; ≥100 lux; irregularities ≤5 mm; passing spaces 1,500 × 1,500 every ≤50 m if narrower than 1,500. Widths: external ≥1,200 (1,800 rec.), internal ≥1,000 (1,200 rec.), turning 1,500.
- Accessible entrances: residential/hotel/business/industrial 1–2 entrances→1, 3–4→2, >4→50%; assembly/mall/retail/education/healthcare/public 100%. Public-building accessible entrances power-operated; revolving doors need adjacent door; ≥1 access gate 900 mm (no turnstile); doormats flush ±5 mm.
- Interaction space 800 × 1,300. Knee clearance 700 h × 800 w; toe 250 h. Protrusions 700–2,100 mm max 100 mm; low headroom areas guarded.
- Reach: 400–1,200 mm (obstructed forward 500–600 deep → 1,100). Children high/low: 3–4 yrs 900/500; 5–8 1,000/450; 9–12 1,100/400. Switches/sockets 400–1,200 (fine controls 750–1,000), ≥300 from corners.
- Doors: clear ≥915 (toilets ≥815); threshold ≤13 mm; force ≤25 N; levers 865–1,220; closers ≥5 s; no knobs. Manoeuvring (Table C.11): front pull 1,500 wide + 450–600 latch side; front push 1,200 + 300; hinge-side pull 1,500 + 900; latch-side pull 1,200 + 600; sliding 1,200 + 300. Doors in series 1,200 + swing. Power doors: ≥3 s open, ≥5 s hold, ≤66 N, controls 900–1,200.
- Ramps: max 8.33% (rec. 5–6% for rise >1 m, ≤7% for 0.5–1 m, ≤8% <0.5 m); runs ≤10 m; cross-fall ≤2%; width ≥1,000 between rails; landings 1,500 × 1,500; doors ≥1,500 from ramp ends; total rise >2.4 m → lift; handrails both sides if rise >300 at 865–965 + 650–750; intermediate rail if >2,100 wide; edge protection. Curved ramps ≤5%, 1,500 wide.
- Lifts: ≥1 accessible lift in every multi-storey building except: 2-storey restaurants with equal amenities (upper ≤30% of dining), plant spaces, G+1 with upper floor <250 m² GA and not public/retail/healthcare, ≤5 occupants upstairs, plots <250 m², mosques with accessible lower level. Doors ≥900 × 2,100; 1,500 × 1,500 landing; buttons 900–1,200 with raised Arabic numerals + Braille; exit floor button raised with green ring; rails 900 ±100; mirror; voice + visual announcements; hands-free intercom. Lift platforms only where lift impossible.
- Curb ramps ≤8%, ≥1,200 wide, curbs 75–150, tactile 300 mm.
- Accessible parking: 25–500 bays 2% (min 1); >500 +1% of excess; cinemas/sport 1 per 2 wheelchair seats; hospitals 10% rec.; vans 10% of accessible (3.35 × 5.5); bays 2.5 × 5.5 + 1.2 m aisle; ≤50 m from accessible entrance; one at each accessible entrance; grade-level bay in multi-level car parks.
- Floors: level change ≤13 mm bevelled, else ramp. Slip PTV (BS EN 13036-4): indoor dry 15–35 (≥35 on slopes ≥5%); outdoor/wet 35–45 (≥45 on slopes); pools/showers/streets ≥45. Carpet pile ≤13 mm.
- Tactile (ISO 23599): warning ≥300 deep set back 300 from hazard; guidance ≥400 wide — in open squares, transport hubs, entrance→reception→lifts in public admin buildings.
- Glass manifestation 750–1,500 mm band, 50% coverage per 900 mm at ≥50% opacity.
- Furniture: 20% lockers 600–1,200; seats 430–480 high; benches every 50 m in malls/airports, 100 m outdoors; counters: one of each type accessible (20% if sales >500 m²), ≤850 high × 900 wide; reception 700–850.
- Accessible toilets: public buildings – wheelchair WC per gender on every floor and every toilet group (or unisex), within 150 m; parks/beaches within 400 m; ≥5% WCs accessible; ≥1 accessible urinal, basin, shower per group; GA >2,000 m² with children → 10% child fixtures. Details: door ≥815 outward/sliding; 1,500 Ø turning; WC seat 450–500; 800 × 1,300 transfer space; grab bars 750–900 long at 700–850; fold-down bar; Ø30–50, 1 kN. Basin rim 800–850, knee 700. Showers: transfer 900 × 900, roll-in 800 × 1,500, seat 450–500. Mirrors ≤1,050 above basin. Baby change in male and female toilets ≤150 m apart. Family toilets in malls/parks/amusement parks within 300 m (2,000 Ø, adult & baby boards, child basin 500). Accessible ablution nearest entrance. Drinking fountains two heights (accessible 750–900).
- Hearing loops/IR required in public receptions, meeting/waiting >25 people, auditoria, counters, theatres, mosques, cinemas, concert halls (receivers: ≤50 seats 2; 51–500 2 + 1/25 over 50; 501–1,000 20 + 1/50; >1,000 40 + 1/100).
- Auditoria wheelchair spaces: 1 per 50 seats to 500, +1 per 100 above; 900 wide (850 paired) × 1,300/1,500; companion seats for 50%; dispersed; stage accessible; >300 spectators → accessible backstage WC + shower.
- Pools >25 m (public): sloped entry ≤8% to 600–750 depth or chair lift. Beaches: 1,500 routes to water, accessible shower per 150 m. Dining: ≥10% accessible tables, route to ≥50% of areas. Hotels: mobility rooms 2% (50–500 rooms) +1% above 500, half roll-in showers, half baths, 1,500 × 1,500 beside bed, bed ≤500 high; communication-feature rooms 4% (+2% above 500). Residential >10 units → 2% adaptable units (Approved Doc M). Offices/industrial fully accessible, aisles 1,000.
- Signage English + Arabic, 50 LRV contrast, ISO 7000 pictograms; wall signs centred 1.5 m; tactile maps 1,250–1,750 (inclined 900–1,200 at 30–45°); raised text 16–50 mm, Braille below; use ≥2 of visual/acoustic/tactile channels.

## Part E – Building envelope
- Structural: resist Part F loads (wind per Part F or tunnel), accommodate drift, durable non-welded tested fixings (life ≥ envelope life), no detachment. Deflection: storey-height framing L/200 (≤3 m), 5 mm + L/300 (3–7.5 m), L/250 (≥7.5 m); stone L/360; metal panels L/90; single glass framing L/125; IGU L/175. Glass ASTM E1300; structural glass redundant. Impact (CWCT TN75/76): ≤1.5 m public 500 J soft / 10 J hard; above 1.5 m 350 J.
- Energy compliance: elemental (E.5.2 + H.4.8.1 + H.7.2) or performance (ASHRAE 90.1-2019 App G, proposed ≤ reference).
- Max WWR: residential 40%, others 60%. Per orientation max glazing of elevation: N 80%, NE/NW 70%, E/W 60%, SE/S/SW 40%.
- U-values: roof ≤0.3 W/m²K; walls & exposed floors ≤0.57; ground slab 1 m perimeter insulation; insulation per FLSC Ch.1.
- Glazing (whole-unit U incl. frame): WWR <40% U ≤2.1, SC ≤0.4, LT ≥40%; 40–60% U ≤1.9, SC ≤0.32, LT ≥32%; >60% U ≤1.7, SC ≤0.25, LT ≥25%. Upper-floor shopfronts U ≤1.9, SC ≤0.76. Skylights U ≤1.9; ≤10% of roof SC ≤0.32 LT ≥32%; >10% SC ≤0.25 LT ≥25%.
- Thermal bridges eliminated/insulated; frames sealed; SRI ≥78 flat roofs / ≥29 steep roofs on ≥75% of roof; walls ≥29. Façade lighting ≤2.2 W/m² or 16.4 W/m; full cut-off fittings; wall-wash spill ≤10%; daylight cut-off.
- Moisture: DPC ≥150 mm above ground; sealed/overlapped horizontal surfaces; drained cavities/rainscreens; flashings at openings, penetrations, junctions; condensation control (thermal breaks, VCL continuity).
- Safety glazing in critical locations: BS EN 12600 class 1 low level, class 2 doors below 900, class 3 side panels above 900 (or ANSI Z97.1/CPSC 16 CFR 1201). Glazing below 800 mm protecting >760 mm drop must give containment. Operable windows sill ≥900, opening restrictor 100 mm, no climbable features. Overhead glazing laminated (CWCT TN66/67).
- Façade fire (FLSC Ch.1 amended): non-combustible exceptions extended to concrete, terracotta, glass, ceramics, mineral wool. Solid metal panels: high-rise, super high-rise, malls, assembly, warehouses, industrial, hospitals → A1/A2-s1-d0 + system test (BS 8414/BR 135, NFPA 285, FM 4881 or ISO 13785-2); mid/low-rise & schools → B-s1-d0 + system test; rated walls → A1/A2 + E119/UL 263/EN 1364. Balcony sprinklers and sprinklers behind curtain walls not required by DCD. Spandrel <915 mm only with listed perimeter barrier. Roofing classes per Table E.13; sloped roofs tested as façades. Signage >50 m² or spanning >1 storey tested with façade. PV per FLSC Ch.14.
- Maintenance: permanent safe access (rope access anchors per IRATA or BMU); lifeline stabilisation every 61 m; anchors 4× load and ≥1,334 N wind; BMU safety features.

## Part F – Structure
- Design life: buildings 50 yr; special (>300 m, monumental, essential) 100 yr; temporary 10 yr. Agree with Owner/Authority.
- Robustness by ASCE 7 risk category (UFC 4-023-03): II tie forces or alternate path; III alternate path + enhanced perimeter ground columns; IV ties + AP + enhanced resistance.
- Durability: BS 8500-1; durability report if life >50 yr, DS-4+, XD3 or XS2+. Steel coatings BS EN 12944 + UV.
- Concrete ACI 318-19: fcu ≥35 MPa (f'c 28); blinding/screed fcu 20; precast fcu 40 (transfer 25). DM green concrete mixes (Table F.3, GGBS/fly-ash) or Dubai Sustainable Concrete Baseline. Covers recommended: 50 mm (on blinding) / 75 mm (against soil) substructure, 30 mm superstructure. Precast topping mesh ≥Ø8@200, services in screed. Steel AISC 360/341 or BS EN grades; plates ≥6 mm. Masonry TMS 402/602 or BS EN 1996; units ≥9 MPa load-bearing, ≥6 MPa non-load-bearing. Fire: ACI 216.1, AISC DG19, FLSC construction types.
- Loads ASCE 7-16: densities RC 25 kN/m³, blockwork 20, AAC 7, steel 78; SDL partitions 0.75 kPa, services+ceiling 0.5, services 0.3; car parks ≥3.5 kPa; pool surrounds 2 kPa; saturated planter soil 19 kN/m³. Design groundwater: near water DMD ±0.00 + 1.0 m; elsewhere measured + 1.0 m. Thermal ±20 °C exposed, ±15 °C internal.
- Wind (3 s gust, 10 m): 1-yr 22 m/s (comfort), 10-yr 30 (drift), 50-yr 38, RC I 44, RC II 47, RC III 51, RC IV 53 m/s; min pressure 1 kPa. Wind tunnel mandatory >120 m, H/B >5 or unusual. Drift (10-yr) H/500 overall, h/400–h/600 storey. Accelerations vs ISO 10137 (1 & 10-yr). Damping concrete 1–2% (SLS), steel 0.75–1%.
- Seismic ASCE 7-16: Ss 0.51, S1 0.18, TL 24 s (site class B, enhanced); Fa/Fv: A 0.8/0.8, B 0.9/0.8, C 1.296/1.5, D 1.392/2.24, E 1.684/site study, F site response; unknown → class D; SDS = ⅔·Fa·Ss, SD1 = ⅔·Fv·S1. Include non-structural components. Mass/stiffness change >10% → reassess (ASCE 41).
- Serviceability: incremental deflection ≤20 mm after finishes; ACI 224R cracks; vibration AISC DG11/TR43; differential shortening analysis for tall/asymmetric/outrigger buildings; seismic gap δMT = √(δM1² + δM2²); avoid below-ground movement joints; avoid transfers (≥2 supports, no eccentricity).
- Geotech (calcareous soils, aggressive saline water): BS EN 1997 + UK NA, BS 5930. Boreholes 1 per 750 m², min 5 (>G+12) or 3 (≤G+12); large sites 60 m grid. Piles FS ≥2.5, verticality 1/75, 75 mm eccentricity, rock socket ≥3D, min 6Ø12 full length; tests: ≥1 preliminary pile, static 1%, dynamic 5%, sonic 10%, integrity 100%. Shoring: temporary <2 yr; watertight types near buildings/high water; surcharge 20 kPa; anchors 3–10 m; max deflection 40 mm. Liquefaction FS ≥1.5, M 6.2, PGA 0.13 × F_PGA. Excavations >1.2 m need permit.

## Part G – Incoming utilities
- DEWA LV 230/400 V TN-S, neutral earthed only at DEWA; 40 kA. Main switchroom at ground near plot entrance (next to transformer room if any); 2 h enclosure (internal door 90 min, non-louvred); door opens out, not onto driveways/stairs; AC; 500 lux; never under/beside wet areas; no pipes through; no storage.
- Meters: villas/single premises in IP55 compound-wall cabinet; multi-tenant meters in electrical rooms; 2 m from water meters; submetering (≥100 kW systems) when cooling ≥1 MW or GFA ≥1,000 m².
- Design ambient 48 °C; voltage drop ≤4%; LSF/XLPE copper cables; BS 1363 sockets; IP55 outdoors. Heights: switches 1.25 m, sockets 450 mm, kitchen sockets 150 mm above worktop (Part C reach applies to accessible areas). Labels English + Arabic.
- Max demand: 1,000 kVA transformer 800 kW (650 kW AC-heavy); 1,500 kVA 1,200 kW (950). Motors >110 kW → DEWA bulk supply.
- Standby generators for malls, hotels, theme parks, museums, major exhibition halls: lifts, escalators, security, BMS, control/electrical rooms, ≥20% landlord lighting; mobile-generator connection for other panels.
- EV charging: Mode 1 prohibited; Mode 2 ≤32 A; Mode 3/4 EVSE on dedicated radials; DEWA approval. PV: grid-connected under Shams Dubai; FLSC Ch.14.
- Substations (11 kV): needed by DEWA for large loads; ground-floor single-room: 1 × 1,000/1,500 kVA 33 m² (4.57 m wide), 2 × 55 m² (6.1 m), +25 m² each; clear height ≥3.7 m; FFL 150–300 above ground. Split: RMU room 9 m² (3 m wide, +7 m² per RMU), height ≥3 m; transformer room 21 m² each. Basement transformers B1 only, with RMU at ground. Open-to-sky 6.1 × 6.1 m. Must front RTA road/sikka (sikka ≥6.1 m, or ≥3 m within 12 m of main road); 24/7 access; no wet areas above (or waterproof RC attic slab); 2 h rated; natural ventilation on 2 sides (transformer louvred door 3.05 × 2.75). Pocket substations not inside buildings or at petrol stations. Towers >200 m may take direct 11 kV (cast-resin transformers).
- LPG FLSC Ch.11; risers in 2 h shaft. Water: DEWA main + tenant submeters. District cooling: coordinate with provider from concept; ETS variable flow, PICVs, 5–100% flow, bypass, flushing.
- Telecom (TRA FTTx): open access du/Etisalat; MTR at ground/basement 2 × 2 × 3 m (small) to 3 × 3 × 3 m; FTR per floor, aligned risers; RTMR/MSR; rooms away from water/heat/EMI; 1 m around cabinets; consolidation cabinet per tenancy.

## Part H – Indoor environment
- Design: outdoor 46 °C DB / 29 °C WB (zone 0B); indoor 24 ±1.5 °C, 50 ±5% RH; comfort 22.5–25.5 °C and 30–60% RH for 95% of year; air speed 0.2–0.3 m/s; treated outdoor air (34 °C DB / 32 °C WB); safety factors ≤10% sensible, ≤5% latent.
- Efficiency: ESMA UAE.S 5010-5; chillers ASHRAE 90.1 Table 6.8.1; energy recovery if OA >1,000 l/s (50% of exhaust, 70% sensible); DCV with CO₂ in spaces >100 m² at ≥25 p/100 m² (setpoint <800 ppm, alarm 1,000); airtightness ≤5 m³/h·m² @50 Pa for cooling ≥1 MW; entrance lobbies/air curtains.
- Access: 762 × 762 service space; plant door path 915 × 2,300; permanent access to roof plant >4.8 m. Fuel appliances not in bedrooms/bathrooms.
- IAQ before occupancy: formaldehyde <0.08 ppm, TVOC <300 µg/m³, PM10 <150 µg/m³; DCL-certified low-VOC products; intakes upwind, exhausts downwind; sand-trap louvres ≤1 m/s.
- Car parks: CO <50 ppm, ≥6 ACH or CO-controlled, sensor per 400 m², alarm 75 ppm, smoke clearance 10 ACH, positive pressure to adjacent occupied spaces. Kitchens: Type I/II hoods, make-up 75–95% of exhaust, kitchen −0.25 Pa. Flues ≥610 mm above roof and anything within 3,050 mm.
- BMS if cooling ≥1,000 kW or GA ≥5,000 m². Hotel rooms: occupancy cut-off of lights/AC/power; AC off when balcony door open.
- Water: max flows shower 8, basin 6, sink 7 l/min; WC 6/3 l; urinal 2.4 l (non-public) / 1 l or waterless (public). Condensate reuse if cooling >350 kW. Non-potable or drip irrigation. Greywater dual plumbing labelled. No DEWA potable water in cooling towers (TSE/seawater). Solar hot water targeting 75% of demand. Storage 24 h (labour accommodation 48 h), ≥1 m³, two compartments; DEWA per-capita l/day: flats 200–300, villas 250–350, hotel guest 200–300, office 45–60, schools 40–60, labour 80–150. GRP tanks ≤3 m high; away from drains. Hot water stored ≥60 °C, 50 °C at outlet within 1 min, return 50–55 °C, TMV ≤45 °C at hygiene outlets.
- Drainage BS EN 12056/752: vented stacks; no connections near stack base (1 floor <20 storeys, 2 floors >20); rodding eyes; pumped stations N+1; rainwater 75 mm/h unless agreed; external grease traps (BS EN 1825); oil interceptors; holding tanks where no sewer (RC/GRP ≥1 m, brick ≥3 m from boundary/building and water tanks).
- Lighting: BS EN 12464-1; LPD W/m²: offices/hotels/restaurants/assembly 7.5, education 7.8, industrial 8.9, retail/malls 9.8, warehouses 4.9, residential common 6.9; exterior: parking 0.86 W/m², façades 2.2 W/m². Controls: corridors auto-dim to 25%; occupancy sensors in offices & schools; daylight dimming within 6 m of office windows (400–500 lux). Egress: stairs ≥108 lux, other routes ≥10.8 lux.
- Commissioning: CIBSE codes; systems manual + user guide.
- Fire systems (FLSC Ch.7–9, DCD notes): voice evacuation in high-rise, super high-rise, malls, assembly, theme parks, schools, hotels, hospitals, detention (speakers in stairs; no sounders in stairs or command centre); firefighter phones in super high-rise, malls, assembly, theme parks, hotels; roof landing valve; floor drainage in basements/corridors.
- Acoustics: residential Approved Document E; schools BB93; healthcare HTM 08-01; offices/assembly/industrial BS 8233 (+BCO); hotels operator or ADE; services noise ASHRAE; acoustic consultant for mosques, performance, studios, cinemas, malls, gyms over dwellings.
- Smart/ICT (only if briefed): layered architecture, open protocols, segregated SP/landlord/tenant networks, ISO/IEC 11801, Cat 6A horizontal, halogen-free Cca-s1b,d2,a2 cables, two MERs ≥30 m apart, shared satellite head-end.
