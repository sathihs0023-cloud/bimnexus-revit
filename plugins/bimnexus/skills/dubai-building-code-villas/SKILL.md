# Dubai Building Code 2021 – Part K: Villas & Townhouses

Part K is a standalone part for detached villas and townhouses up to 3 storeys. Villas converted to retail, restaurants, clinics or other non-residential uses follow Parts A–J (use `dubai-building-code-architecture` / `dubai-building-code-technical`). Part A rules still apply: villa basements excluded from GFA; DCR/affection plan overrides defaults below.

## How to use
- Check the plot DCR/affection plan first (setbacks, height, coverage, parking, datum). Quote DBC clause refs (K.x). These are paraphrased notes — confirm against the official latest DBC and the permitting Authority (DM/DDA/Trakhees/DSOA, DEWA, DCD).
- For design reviews, produce a checklist: item | DBC ref | requirement | design | OK?

## Planning & massing (K.5.1–K.5.2)
- Gate level ≥ +300 mm above road edge or 2–5% slope.
- Height from road edge level at main access to average roof FFL; total height max 16 m.
- Default setbacks (Table K.1): villa — road 3 m, neighbour 3 m, sikka 3 m; townhouse/attached — road 3 m, neighbour 0, sikka 3 m. Several buildings on one plot ≥1.5 m apart. Separation or fire-rated external walls per FLSC Ch.1.
- Annexes (garage, maid's room, outdoor kitchen, gym, enclosed pool, majlis): may sit on the boundary or follow setbacks; ≥1.5 m from main villa (townhouse annex may attach); area ≤60% of main building GA; height ≤5.5 m.
- In setbacks allowed: fence, landscape, basement/substructure, pergolas & light parking shades, internal roads, parking, annexes.
- Balconies/projections: ≥2.7 m above FFL, within setbacks, never beyond plot; decorative projections ≤300 mm into setback (≥2.7 m up), ≤900 mm at roof; ≥3 m from neighbouring villas/plot limits; mind privacy.
- Guardrails where drop >760 mm: ≥1,200 mm; 100 mm sphere; no climbable rails within 865 mm; bottom gap ≤100 mm; ASCE 7-16 loads; glass balustrades laminated.
- GA: inside external walls (townhouse to party-wall centreline). Plot coverage from external wall faces incl. roofed balconies; excludes canopies, parking shades, pergolas, eaves.
- Basement within plot; soffit ≤1.5 m above gate level; habitable basement rooms need daylight. Roof structures ≤50% of roof and set back ≥1.5 m (stair/lift rooms exempt from setback, count in 50%), otherwise counted as a floor; roof guardrails, waterproofing, insulation.

## Spaces & openings (K.5.3–K.5.4)
- Bedrooms/living ≥10.5 m², min dimension 3 m; housekeeper/guard room ≥5.5 m² (2.1 m), excl. toilet; enclosed kitchen ≥5.4 m² (1.8 m); 1.2 m between kitchen counters.
- Clear heights: living & bedrooms 2.7 m; other spaces 2.3; stairs 2.3; non-habitable rooms under stairs 2.03.
- Doors: entry ≥915 mm; bathrooms/toilets ≥710 mm.
- Windows: bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens net glazing ≥10% of floor area (or daylight model ≥150 lux); housekeeper rooms ≥8%; sill ≥900 mm; mechanical openers 900–1,500; skylights ≥15° count; room depth ≤3× width of window wall; views from habitable rooms; windows facing neighbours only with setback + FLSC fire spread.

## Circulation (K.5.5–K.5.6)
- Corridors ≥1.0 m, headroom 2.3 m.
- Stairs ≥1,000 mm clear (handrail may project 100 mm); risers 100–180 mm; treads ≥280 mm; ≤10 mm variation; landings ≥1,000 mm (no intermediate landing required; recommended if floor-to-floor >4 m); door swing ≤½ landing; headroom 2.3 m. Spiral, winder and open stairs allowed except for basement exit — basement needs an enclosed 1,000 mm stair to discharge level. Spiral: width ≥1,000, headroom ≥2,030, riser ≤240, tread ≥190 at 305 mm line (not recommended as main stair); curved: headroom 2,300, tread ≥255 at 305; winders ≥150 narrow / ≥280 at 305.
- Handrail on open side at 865–965 mm (≤1,065 where part of guard); Ø32–51; 57 mm wall gap; extensions; guard ≥1,200 (1,065 where handrail forms guard); 100 mm sphere to 865; 150 mm triangle. External stairs >11 m → 1,200 opaque screen. Egress per FLSC Ch.3; ramps per C.5.9.1; lifts per D.5/D.6.
- Vehicle access ≥6 m from chamfered plot corner at intersections, not opposite a T-junction, RTA approval. Cluster housing: 6 m fire access within 60 m of all buildings.
- Ramps: straight ≤12%, lane 3 m, headroom 2.4 m; curved ≤12%, 3.5 m, R ≥4 m; helical ≤8%, 5 m, R ≥6 m; single-lane ramp allowed; anti-slip, mirrors, drainage.
- Parking ≥1 bay per villa/townhouse (DCR prevails); tandem OK; slope ≤4%. Garages: single open 3.0 × 5.5 m; single enclosed 3.6 × 6.0; double enclosed 6.0 × 6.0. More than 2 bays → B.7.2.4 bay/aisle sizes.

## Sanitary, amenities, waste (K.5.7–K.5.13)
- Every dwelling: WC, basin, shower or bath. Wet finishes: 100 mm coved base; impervious walls to 1,200 within 600 mm of fixtures; shower walls to 1,800. Fixture centres ≥400 from walls, 750 between; 750 in front; 600 to opposite fixture.
- Gyms: consider neighbour noise. Private pools (DM pool safety guide): shallow-end depth 850–1,200 mm; floor slope ≤12% shallow, ≤33% deep; lighting above & under water; windows by pool sill ≥1,200; fence ≥1,200, ≤50 mm gap, 100 mm sphere, self-closing/latching lockable gates opening outward (exemptions: house walls with alarmed/self-closing doors, ASTM F1346 cover, natural water body, pool wall ≥1,200 high with lockable/removable ladder); 1.2 m deck at 2%. Community pools → B.8.3.2.
- Waste: on-plot area with ≥1 × 240 l general + 1 × 240 l recycling (max 360 l) or roofed communal store (bulk ≤2,500 l); residents ≤50 m travel; collectors ≤30 m (≤240 l) / 15 m (>240 l); ≤1:12; store 2 days at 2.5 kg/bedroom/day; 150 mm between bins; ventilated, insect-screened, out-swinging self-closing doors.
- Townhouse party walls 2 h fire-rated foundation-to-roof (1 h if sprinklered); unprotected openings per FLSC Table 1.4; finishes FLSC 2.14. Screen outdoor plant. ≥50% hardscape SRI ≥29 / open grid / shaded; shielded landscape lighting.
- Accessibility optional; if provided follow Part C + Approved Document M Vol 1.

## Envelope (K.7)
- Elemental or performance (ASHRAE 90.1 App G) route.
- U-values: roof ≤0.3, walls & exposed floors ≤0.57 W/m²K; 1 m perimeter slab insulation; if columns/beams are uninsulated, improve glazing U by 10%.
- Glazing by WWR: <40% U ≤2.1, SC ≤0.4, LT ≥40%; 40–60% U ≤1.9, SC ≤0.32, LT ≥32%; >60% U ≤1.7, SC ≤0.25, LT ≥25%. Skylights U ≤1.9 (SC 0.32/0.25 for ≤10%/>10% of roof).
- Thermal bridges treated; frames sealed; roof SRI ≥78 flat / ≥29 steep on 75% of roof; walls ≥29; DPC ≥150 mm; flashings; safety glazing & containment as Part E (E.9); roofing FLSC s6; PV FLSC Ch.14; exterior lights full cut-off, wall-wash spill ≤10%.

## Structure & geotech (K.8)
- Design life 50 yr; indirect robustness (ASCE 7 App C); ACI 318-19, AISC 360/341, TMS 402/602 or BS EN 1996; loads ASCE 7-16 (partitions 0.75 kPa, services+ceiling 0.5, garage 3.5 kPa, pool surround 2 kPa); incremental deflection ≤20 mm; design water table DMD+1.0 m near water; seismic/wind as Part F.
- Boreholes: single villa ≥2; communities 1 per plot. Piles/shoring/liquefaction as Part F.

## Utilities & indoor environment (K.9–K.10)
- DEWA meter cabinet recessed in compound wall (IP55), 2 m from water meter; BS 7671; switches 1.25 m, sockets 450 mm, kitchen sockets 150 mm above worktop; English/Arabic labels. Telecom: ≥2 service providers, Cat 6 min (Cat 6A rec.), ≥4 fibre cores per villa, 2 × 50 mm entry ducts, consolidation cabinet.
- HVAC: outdoor 46/29 °C; comfort 22.5–25.5 °C, 30–60% RH; treated outdoor air; energy modelling for villas >2,000 m²; ASHRAE 62.2 ventilation; bathroom fans interlocked to lights/sensors; occupancy sensors in corridors and bathrooms; exterior lighting off in daylight.
- Water: shower 8, basin 6, sink 7 l/min; WC 6/3 l; storage ≥24 h (250–350 l/person/day), optional 50/50 split tank; pool via break tank; outlet temps shower ≤41 °C, basin 38–41, bath 44 (TMVs); solar thermal or heat-pump hot water mandatory.
- Drainage: floor drains in all wet areas (75 mm seal, trap primer if no regular flow); stacks/vents ≥3 m from openings and intakes, ≥2 m above roof; uPVC BS EN 1329-1; no drainage through electrical rooms, kitchens, bedrooms; min sizes WC 100, basin 32, sink/shower 40, floor drain 75, SVP 100, RWP 100, AC drain 32; access points every 12–20 m; basement sump pits ≥1 m deep with duty/standby pumps; manholes inside plot, final inspection chamber by compound wall; manholes ≤1.3 m deep 600 × 600, 1.3–1.7 m 800 × 800, 1.7–2.5 m Ø1,000 GRP-lined.
- Rainwater: gutters 1/50–1/70; discharge to ground/road, not sewer or neighbours, ≥2 m from building; paved areas 65 mm/h. No sewer → holding tank ≥25 m³ (RC/GRP ≥1 m from buildings/boundary); soakaways for surface water only, ≥3 m from footings/boundary, ≥1 m above water table, ≥1 m from septic tank.
- Fire: smoke detection in all villas linked to DCD via Hassantuk; basement emergency lighting; sprinklers + hose reels if ground floor GA >1,500 m² (every room) or basement GA >1,500 m² (whole basement); no dry risers needed.
- Acoustics: Approved Document E (party walls/floors, bedrooms).
- Security: community common areas per SIRA (Part J).
